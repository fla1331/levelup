List: 15 Free PC Games That Deserve a Chance

Time LevelUp 17/09/2026 4 min read

15 Free PC Games That Deserve a Chance in 2024

Who says quality entertainment has to be expensive? The internet is full of amazing games you can download without spending a dime, and many offer experiences just as good as paid titles. If you're looking for new adventures without breaking the bank, you've come to the right place!

In this list, we've gathered 15 free PC games that are truly worth your time. These include diverse titles, from competitive battle royales to emotional stories and relaxing experiences. The best part? All are available for immediate download.

We've selected options for all tastes: strategy lovers, action fanatics, deep RPGs, and even quirky simulators. What's more, several of these games are among the best free PC games available today.

Get your mouse and keyboard (or controller) ready because this selection will surprise you!

1. Genshin Impact

This anime-style open-world action RPG has won millions over with its stunning visuals and addictive gameplay. Explore Teyvat, collect unique characters, and unravel epic mysteries - all without spending anything.

2. Warframe

One of the best free-to-play shooters, Warframe casts you as a Tenno, a futuristic warrior with incredible abilities. The customization is deep, and the combat is extremely satisfying.

3. Path of Exile

Considered by many as the true spiritual successor to Diablo II, this ARPG features a complex skill system and challenging endgame content. Perfect for loot enthusiasts and creative build crafters.

4. Dota 2

Valve's MOBA remains one of the most popular esports. With hundreds of unique heroes and intense matches, Dota 2 is completely free - you only pay for cosmetic items.

5. Team Fortress 2

This classic class-based shooter remains fun after over 15 years. With nine distinct characters and varied game modes, TF2 proves that simplicity and personality still work.

6. Epic Battle Fantasy 5

A turn-based RPG packed with humor and pop culture references. Despite its simple visuals, it offers deep combat mechanics and dozens of hours of content.

7. Alien Swarm: Reactive Drop

Cooperation is key in this top-down shooter from Valve. Gather up to 8 friends to face alien hordes in tactical, intense missions.

8. Dauntless

Inspired by Monster Hunter but with a more accessible approach. Hunt colossal beasts alone or in teams in this stylish action-RPG.

9. Paladins

A hero shooter that blends MOBA elements with FPS gameplay. With a diverse roster of champions and a unique card system, it offers a fresh take on the genre.

10. Brawlhalla

This platform fighter is perfect for Super Smash Bros. fans who prefer something simpler and free. With cross-play between platforms, there's always someone to challenge.

11. Hearthstone

Blizzard's card game continues to attract millions with regular expansions and innovative modes. The basic version is completely free and offers hours of strategic fun.

12. Splitgate

Imagine Halo with portals - that's essentially what this FPS delivers. The unique teleportation system adds an incredible strategic layer to matches.

13. War Thunder

The most comprehensive free military combat simulator available. Control tanks, planes, and ships in massive battles with realistic physics.

14. Legends of Runeterra

The card game set in the League of Legends universe stands out for its generous model and innovative mechanics. Build creative decks without spending a fortune.

15. Valorant

Riot Games' tactical FPS combines CS:GO's precision with special abilities. Short, intense matches perfect for playing with friends.

As you can see, the world of free games is full of amazing options that rival (and often surpass) paid titles. From immersive solo experiences to fierce online competitions, there's something for everyone.

What's most interesting is that many of these games constantly evolve, with new content added regularly. If you're tired of spending fortunes on new releases or simply want to try something new without commitment, these 15 titles are excellent starting points.

Can I play these free games on a low-end PC?

Yes! Many titles on the list, like Team Fortress 2, Brawlhalla, and Paladins, run on modest configurations. Always check the minimum requirements on the game's page before downloading.

Are these games pay-to-win?

Most listed titles follow a fair free-to-play model where you only pay for cosmetic items or convenience. Games like Path of Exile and Legends of Runeterra are especially praised for their balanced economic models.

What's the best free game for solo play?

For solo experiences, we recommend Genshin Impact (RPG), Path of Exile (ARPG), or Epic Battle Fantasy 5 (turn-based RPG). All offer dozens of hours of content without needing multiplayer.

Do I need to create an account to play?

Most require some registration, especially online games. However, many allow logins via Steam, Epic Games, or other known platforms, making the process easier.

How do I download these games?

All are available on Steam (store.steampowered.com), the Epic Games Store, or the developers' official websites. Just visit the game's page and click "Install" or "Play Free."
