List: Games Similar to GTA: 12 Alternatives for Open World Fans

Time LevelUp 07/09/2026 4 min read

Games Similar to GTA: 12 Best Open World Alternatives

Introduction

The Grand Theft Auto franchise revolutionized the open-world game genre with its freedom of action, engaging narratives, and cities full of possibilities. If you've already explored everything GTAs have to offer and are looking for similar experiences, we have good news!

The market offers various options that capture the essence of GTA with unique approaches - from established franchises to innovative indie titles. In this list, we've selected the 12 best games that every GTA fan should try.

Get ready to discover new virtual cities where adventure, chaos, and the freedom of exploration await you! And if you want more options, check out our complete list of the best open-world games.

The 12 Best GTA-Style Games

1. Saints Row (Series)

Starting as a more relaxed alternative to GTA, the Saints Row series evolved into a unique identity with its sharp humor, deep customization, and absurd activities. The 2022 reboot maintains the irreverent spirit that made the franchise famous.

2. Watch Dogs (Series)

With an innovative technological approach, Ubisoft's Watch Dogs series features meticulously recreated cities (like Chicago and London) where the ability to hack systems adds strategic layers to traditional open-world mechanics.

3. Mafia (Series)

For those who value deep narratives and historical settings, the Mafia series offers cinematic experiences in the world of organized crime. Mafia: Definitive Edition stands out for its immersive portrayal of the 1930s.

4. Sleeping Dogs

This underrated title combines intense shootouts with a martial arts-inspired combat system, all set in vibrant Hong Kong. The story of infiltration into the Chinese underworld is one of the most captivating in the genre.

5. Red Dead Redemption 2

From Rockstar, the creator of GTA, RDR2 transports players to the Old West with an unprecedented level of detail. The thrilling narrative and living world make this one of the best open-world experiences ever created.

6. Cyberpunk 2077

After significant updates, Night City finally realizes its potential as one of the richest and most immersive open worlds. The blend of RPG and first-person action offers a unique experience in the genre.

7. Yakuza (Series)

With smaller yet extremely dense maps, the Yakuza series balances criminal drama with eccentric activities. Kamurocho, based on the real-life Kabukicho district in Tokyo, is one of the most memorable settings in gaming.

8. Just Cause (Series)

For those who prioritize creative freedom and mass destruction, Just Cause offers exaggerated physics and unique tools like the iconic grappling hook and parachute, allowing for spectacular movements across the open world.

9. The Godfather (Game)

This 2006 classic remains relevant with its territory influence system and progression up the mafia hierarchy. A great option for those seeking the classic atmosphere of organized crime.

10. Sunset Overdrive

A vibrant open world that combines parkour elements with frenetic combat against hordes of mutants. Insomniac Games' characteristic humor shines in this unique experience.

11. Lego City Undercover

The family-friendly version of the genre, perfect for younger players or casual sessions. The Lego world is surprisingly vast and full of clever references and fun missions.

12. Mad Max

In the arid post-apocalyptic world of Mad Max, the focus is on intense vehicular combat and customizing your Magnum Opus. The vast desert hides secrets and challenges for explorers.
