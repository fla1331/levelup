Guide: What is DPS, Tank, Support, and Carry? Learn the Main Terms

What is DPS, Tank, Support, and Carry? Learn the Main Gaming Terms

Introduction

If you're entering the world of video games or looking to deepen your knowledge, understanding basic terminology is essential. The terms DPS, Tank, Support, and Carry represent fundamental concepts present in various genres, particularly in MOBAs, FPS, and MMORPGs. Mastering these roles can be the difference between victory and defeat.

In this comprehensive guide, you'll learn the precise meaning of each term, how to identify these roles in games, and the best way to utilize them in a team. Additionally, we'll cover common mistakes and advanced strategies for those looking to excel.

Whether you're a beginner learning the basics or an experienced player looking to refine your strategies, this article is for you. Keep reading to master these concepts and elevate your gameplay!

Also explore our gamer dictionary to expand your vocabulary in the gaming universe.

Chapter 1: Fundamental Concepts

What is DPS?

DPS stands for "Damage Per Second" and refers to characters or weapons specialized in dealing high damage in short time intervals. Essential for eliminating enemies, they typically have low resistance and require protection.

What is a Tank?

The Tank acts as the team's frontline, with high health and defense to absorb damage and protect allies. While their offensive potential is limited, their presence is vital to allow DPS to operate safely.

What is Support?

Support characters focus on assisting the team through heals, buffs (enhancements), and area control. While not primary damage dealers, their role is crucial for team sustainability.

What is a Carry?

The Carry is the character who assumes responsibility for leading the team to victory, typically a DPS that becomes extremely powerful in the late game. Requires early protection but can dominate when properly developed.

Chapter 2: How to Identify and Utilize Each Role

  1. Analyze the game type: Identify if it's a MOBA (like League of Legends), FPS (like Overwatch), or MMORPG (like World of Warcraft)
  2. Recognize attributes: High-damage characters are DPS, high-health are Tanks, healing abilities indicate Supports
  3. Understand synergies: Tanks protect DPS, Supports assist Tanks and DPS, Carries need time to become strong
  4. Adapt your playstyle: As Tank, position in front; as Support, stay in back; as DPS, choose safe positions
  5. Communicate with team: Inform your role and needs ("Need protection to farm" as Carry)

To deepen your knowledge about team strategies, check out our guide on teamwork in games.

Chapter 3: Advanced Strategies

  • DPS: Master tactical positioning and priority target selection
  • Tank: Learn to control spaces and use crowd control (CC) abilities
  • Support: Optimize time between healing allies and disrupting enemies
  • Carry: Know when to focus on farming (resource collection) and when to engage
  • All: Track the current game meta to identify most effective characters

Some games allow role combinations like Tank/Support or DPS/Carry, so experiment with different approaches!

Common Mistakes and How to Avoid Them

  • DPS on frontline: DPS should attack from distance or after Tank engages
  • Overly defensive Tanks: Tanks should create space and initiate fights
  • Damage-focused Supports: Priority should be assisting allies
  • Carry engaging too early: Focus on farming until reaching potential
  • Ignoring teamwork: All roles need cooperation for success

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a character perform multiple roles simultaneously?

Yes! Some characters are hybrids, like Tank-Supports (Braum in LoL) or DPS-Carries (Vayne in LoL). This depends on the character's inherent abilities and adopted playstyle.

What's the difference between DPS and Carry?

Every Carry is DPS, but not all DPS are Carries. Carries are DPS that start weak but become extremely powerful late-game, while other DPS may maintain stable performance throughout.

Is success possible without Tank or Support?

In casual matches it might work, but in competitive play the absence of these roles typically results in imbalance and higher chances of defeat.

How to discover which role best fits my style?

Try all roles! If you prefer direct confrontation, Tanks may be ideal. If you enjoy strategy, test Supports. If you want to eliminate opponents quickly, DPS might be your style. Carries require patience and long-term vision.

Conclusion

Now that you understand the main terms DPS, Tank, Support, and Carry, you're ready to apply them in your favorite games. Remember that success depends on efficient combination of these roles and team member cooperation.

As a next step, we recommend practicing each role in different games to understand their particularities. Each title has its specifics, but these fundamental concepts apply to most competitive games.
