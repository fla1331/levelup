List: Best Games to Play with Friends

Time LevelUp 10/09/2026 4 min read

Best Games to Play with Friends: Guaranteed Group Fun!

Playing with friends is one of the best ways to have fun, whether in person or online. If you're looking for multiplayer games to enjoy with your crew, this list will help you choose the best titles for different types of experiences.

We've selected options for all tastes: from casual party games to more elaborate cooperative titles. Whether you want guaranteed laughs or team challenges, we have perfect recommendations for your group.

Many of these games are great for those who enjoy cooperative games or want to test friendships in heated competitions. Fun is guaranteed with any of these choices!

Check out our complete selection and get ready for hours of entertainment with friends. Let's go?

1. Among Us

The phenomenon that became a craze among friend groups! In this social deduction game, players are crew members on a spaceship, but some are secret impostors trying to sabotage the mission. Perfect for groups of 4 to 10 players who love bluffing and solving mysteries.

2. Overcooked 2

Kitchen chaos has never been so fun! In this cooperative game, you and your friends must work together to prepare meals under pressure. Communication and teamwork are essential - and the laughs are guaranteed.

3. Mario Kart 8 Deluxe

The classic racing game everyone loves! With colorful tracks, fun power-ups, and the ability to play with up to 4 players locally (or 12 online), it's perfect for quick and competitive matches.

4. Jackbox Party Pack (any edition)

A series of party games where participants use their smartphones as controllers. With various minigames involving drawing, creative writing, and charades, it's ideal for large groups full of personality.

5. Fortnite

The most popular battle royale right now, perfect for squads of up to 4 players. Build, shoot, and survive while having fun with friends. The no-build mode is great for beginners.

6. Minecraft

The famous building and survival game that has won millions over. Play cooperatively to build amazing worlds or challenge your friends in customized minigames. Versatility is key!

7. Mortal Kombat 11

For those who prefer fighting games, this classic franchise offers intense, stylish combat. Challenge your friends in 1v1 duels with a diverse roster of characters.

8. Rocket League

Soccer with flying cars - simply addictive! Play in teams of 2v2 or 3v3 in this unique esport that combines mechanical skill and teamwork.

9. It Takes Two

Exclusively cooperative, this game requires two players to work together to progress through a story full of creative mechanics and original puzzles. An unforgettable experience for duos.

10. Uno

The classic digital card game everyone knows but never gets old. Play online with up to 4 friends, use special power-ups, and get ready for many exciting twists.

Results and Conclusion

Regardless of your group size or gaming preferences, this list offers options for all types of players. From those seeking cooperative experiences to those who prefer intense competition, there are games for every occasion.

Many of these titles are accessible on various platforms, allowing groups with different systems to play together. What matters is choosing what best suits your friend group's personality.

So, which of these items did you like the most? Share in the comments!

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the best game for very large groups?

For groups larger than 8 people, we recommend the Jackbox Party Pack series, where everyone can participate using their smartphones as controllers. Among Us also works well with up to 10 players.

Are there free options to play with friends?

Yes! Among Us has a free mobile version (with ads), Fortnite is completely free, and Rocket League is also free to play.

What's the best cooperative game for beginners?

Overcooked 2 and It Takes Two are excellent cooperative options for beginners, with simple mechanics to learn but offering progressive challenges.

Do we need to be in the same place to play?

Not necessarily! Most games listed offer online options, allowing friends to play together even at a distance. Some like Mario Kart 8 and Overcooked also allow local multiplayer.
