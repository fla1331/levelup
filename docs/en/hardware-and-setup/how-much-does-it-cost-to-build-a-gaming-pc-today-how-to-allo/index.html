Guide: How Much Does It Cost to Build a Gaming PC Today? How to Allocate the Budget

Time LevelUp 13/09/2026 4 min read

How Much Does It Cost to Build a Gaming PC Today? How to Allocate the Budget

INTRODUCTION

Building a gaming PC can seem like a complex task, especially when it comes to balancing performance and costs. This guide has been developed to help you understand the main aspects involved in assembling a gaming computer, from basic concepts to advanced tips for optimizing your budget.

Here, you will learn how to choose the right parts, allocate your budget efficiently, and avoid common mistakes that could compromise the performance of your equipment. Additionally, we will provide a useful table with information on prices and specifications to facilitate your decision-making process.

Whether you are a beginner or an experienced enthusiast, this guide has something for everyone. Let's start with the fundamental concepts and then move on to a detailed step-by-step guide for assembling your gaming PC.

CHAPTER 1: THE BASICS

Before diving into assembling your gaming PC, it's important to understand some basic concepts. The first step is to familiarize yourself with the main components that make up a gaming computer:

  • Processor (CPU): The brain of the computer, responsible for executing all tasks.
  • Graphics Card (GPU): Responsible for graphics processing, essential for gaming.
  • RAM: Stores temporary data for quick access.
  • Storage (SSD/HDD): Where your files and operating systems are stored.
  • Motherboard: Connects all the computer parts.
  • Power Supply: Provides power to all components.
  • Case: The "house" of your PC, where all parts are installed.

Understanding these components and their functions is crucial for making informed decisions when building your gaming PC. For more details, check out our article on the best components for a gaming PC.

CHAPTER 2: STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE

Now that you know the basics, let's move on to the step-by-step guide for building your gaming PC:

  1. Define your budget: Before buying any parts, it's important to know how much you can spend. A gaming PC can range from R$ 3,000 to R$ 15,000 or more, depending on the specifications.
  2. Choose the processor: Depending on your budget, choose a processor that meets your needs. For gaming, Intel Core i5 or AMD Ryzen 5 processors are good options.
  3. Select the graphics card: The GPU is one of the most important components for gaming. Consider models from NVIDIA or AMD, such as the GeForce RTX 3060 or the Radeon RX 6700 XT.
  4. Choose the RAM: For modern games, 16GB of RAM is recommended.
  5. Decide on storage: A 500GB SSD is ideal for the operating system and some games. You can complement it with an HDD for additional storage.
  6. Select the motherboard: Choose a motherboard that is compatible with the processor and has enough features for your needs.
  7. Choose the power supply: Make sure the power supply has enough wattage to power all components.
  8. Select the case: Choose a case that fits all the parts and has good ventilation.

By following these steps, you will have a gaming PC assembled and ready to play. For more details on each component, check out our article on the best components for a gaming PC.

CHAPTER 3: ADVANCED TIPS

For those who want to take their gaming PC to the next level, here are some advanced tips:

  • Overclocking: Increase the speed of your processor and GPU for more performance. But be careful, this can increase heat generation and require a better cooling system.
  • Liquid cooling: If you plan to overclock or have a very powerful system, consider using a liquid cooling system to keep temperatures low.
  • NVMe SSD: For even faster storage, consider using an NVMe SSD, which offers superior read and write speeds compared to traditional SSDs.
  • RGB lighting: For a more impressive look, add RGB lighting to your case and components.

These tips can help further improve the performance and appearance of your gaming PC.

COMMON MISTAKES AND HOW TO AVOID THEM

  • Not researching prices: Comparing prices across different stores can save a lot of money.
  • Ignoring compatibility: Make sure all parts are compatible with each other, especially the motherboard and processor.
  • Saving on the power supply: A quality power supply is essential for the safety and stability of the system.
  • Not considering cooling: An inadequate cooling system can cause overheating and reduce the lifespan of components.

FAQ

How much does it cost to build a basic gaming PC?

A basic gaming PC can cost between R$ 3,000 and R$ 5,000, depending on the chosen specifications.

What is the difference between SSD and HDD?

SSDs are faster and quieter, while HDDs offer more storage space at a lower cost.

Can I build a gaming PC without a dedicated graphics card?

Yes, but gaming performance will be limited. Processors with integrated graphics can run less demanding games.

How much RAM is needed for gaming?

16GB of RAM is recommended for most modern games.

CONCLUSION

Now that you have all the necessary information, it's time to start building your gaming PC. Remember to define your budget, choose the right parts, and avoid the common mistakes mentioned in this guide.
